## About Me


![Profile Picture]({{ "assets/images/jj.jpg" | relative_url }}){: class="profile-image"}

 
My name is {{ site.name }}, I am a Junior Mechanical Engineering student at Cornell University intested in working in Aerospace. During my time as a student at Cornell, I have developed experience designing, analyzing, optimizing, and manufacturing mechanical components using Fusion 360, SolidWorks, and ANSYS. Outside of classes, I am a member of the Cornell Cup Robotics project team, where my work has included redesigning mounts for electrical components, researching ways to waterproof cable entry ports for our submersible, and redesigning our ballast system to increase the amount of ballast by 50%. Some of my other projects include performing FEA and parametric optimization to redesign a bicycle frame, designing and building a glider from scratch using XFLR5 and Fusion 360. I also have practical manufacturing experience with manual mills, lathes, 3D printing, and laser cutting. I am also in the process of getting certified on the CNC mills and Lathes at Cornell. In addition to my technical experience, my roles as a teaching assistant have strengthened my ability to communicate technical concepts, collaborate with others, and solve problems effectively.

### Project Description 
This was a class project completed for MAE 3050 Introduction to Aeronautics to design and build a glider. The glider had to be designed to be launchable from a standard spring powered launcher at a height of 5 meters. The goal of the project was to make the glider fly 20 meters as slowly as possible.

### Initial Plan
Our initial plan was to design the glider so that we could achieve a glide angle that left our glider with 1 meter of height as a factor of saftey when it crossed the finish line to account for effects we couldn't consider in our calculations since landing before crossing 20 meters would lead to our glider being disqualified. This condition sets our minimum Coefficent of Lift over Coefficent of Drag at a value of 5.

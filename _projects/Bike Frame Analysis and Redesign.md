### Project Description 
This was a class project completed for MAE 3150 Engineering Simulations and Design to complete a FEA analysis on a given bike geometry and then to redesign it to be amenable to use in a bike share program. The other requirements for the project were to maintain a factor of saftey above 2 and to minimize the weight of the bike frame.
